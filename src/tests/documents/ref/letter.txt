Dear Mr. Babbage,

This letter exercises the letter style: the address of the sender and the date flushed to the right, the
address of the recipient on the left, the opening, the body, the closing and the signature, followed
by the copies and the enclosures.

The body of a letter is set as ordinary paragraphs. This second paragraph is long enough to wrap
onto a second line, so that the measure of the letter style and the justification of its lines can be
checked.

Yours sincerely,

Ada Lovelace

Cc: Mary Somerville

Encl: Notes on the Analytical Engine
