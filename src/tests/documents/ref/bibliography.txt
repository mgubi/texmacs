1 Citations

This document cites entries of the file bibliography.bib, which lies next to it, and lets the
bibliography be generated in the tm-plain style. Line breaking is due to Knuth and Plass [2], and
the algorithm is described in the TEXbook [1]. LATEX is documented in [3], and TEXMACS in [4].
Several entries may be cited at once [1, 3], and a citation may carry a detail [1, chapter 14]. An
entry which is never cited does not appear in the bibliography.

Bibliography

[1]

[2]
Donald E. Knuth and Michael F. Plass. Breaking paragraphs into lines. Software: Practice and Experience,
11(11):1119–1184, 1981.
[3]
Leslie Lamport. LATEX: a document preparation system. Addison-Wesley, Second edition, 1994.
[4]
Joris van der Hoeven. GNU TeXmacs: a free, structured, wysiwyg and technical text editor. In Proceedings of
the GUTenberg conference, number 39–40, pages 39–50. Metz, 2001.
