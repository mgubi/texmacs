1 Words

A ligature joins two letters into one glyph, and kerning moves a pair of letters closer together or
further apart. A glyph is drawn by a font. Fonts come in families and shapes, such as the italic
shape.

2 Lines

Paragraphs are broken into lines and lines into pages. Kerning also applies across a line, and every
font has a design size. The measure is the width of the column, and the leading its vertical spacing.

3 Pages

A page holds a number of lines, and its footnotes. A widow is the last line of a paragraph left alone
at the top of a page.
