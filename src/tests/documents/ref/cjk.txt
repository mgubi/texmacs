1 Chinese, Japanese and Korean

Chinese text in the default font, whose ideographs come from a system font found by the font
fallback (on macOS, Songti):

中文排版允许在任意两个汉字之间断行，但句号、逗号等标点不能出现在行首。这一段文字足够
长，可以排成好几行，用来检查断行的规则。

Japanese text in the Hiragino Kaku font of the Document > Font menu, with kanji, hiragana
and katakana:

日本語の文章は、漢字とひらがなとカタカナを混ぜて書きます。テキストが長くなると、行
の終わりで自動的に改行されます。

Korean text in the Gothic font (AppleGothic) of the same menu:
한국어문장입니다.
Ideographs may also occur in a Latin paragraph, as in 中文(Chinese) and 日本(Japan).
