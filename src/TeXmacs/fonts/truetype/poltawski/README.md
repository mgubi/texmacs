# Antykwa Poltawskiego

Sixteen of the forty faces of Antykwa Poltawskiego: the regular and the
light weight at the normal width, and the regular weight condensed and
expanded (the semi-condensed, semi-expanded and the other light widths
are left out).

Upstream: http://www.gust.org.pl/projects/e-foundry/poltawski and
https://ctan.org/pkg/poltawski
