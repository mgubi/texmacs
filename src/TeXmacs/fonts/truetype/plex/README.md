# IBM Plex

IBMPlexMath-Regular.otf with the whole of IBM Plex Sans, Serif, Mono and
Sans Condensed: eight weights (thin, extra light, light, regular, text,
medium, semi bold and bold), each upright and italic.

The files are those of TeX Live, whose names of the families are
shortened (IBM Plex Sans Cond, ExtLt, Medm, SmBld); the entries of
`TeXmacs/fonts/font-features.scm` put them in the master IBM Plex, the
medium weight in a master of its own, IBM Plex Medium.

Upstream: https://github.com/IBM/plex and https://ctan.org/pkg/plex
