# New Computer Modern

NewCMMath-Regular.otf, NewCMMath-Bold.otf and the 10 pt text faces; a
modern extension of Computer Modern with complete symbol coverage and a
bold math font. NewCMSansMath-Regular.otf with the 10 pt sans serif and
typewriter faces gives the sans serif selection (Computer Modern Sans),
and the sans serif and typewriter companions of New Computer Modern.

Upstream: https://ctan.org/pkg/newcomputermodern
