TeXmacs Upsilon downloads

TeXmacs Upsilon is an enhanced and bleeding-edge version of GNU TeXmacs with experimental features (what it adds). These are its latest builds, made by the continuous integration of the repository at each change: they are tested, but they are not releases. Problems go to the issues of the repository, not to the official TeXmacs.
